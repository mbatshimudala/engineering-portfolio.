M28 Cork to Ringaskiddy Motorway

Road Infrastructure | Engineering Surveying | Machine Control

Project Overview

Engineering surveying and digital construction support for motorway paving operations on the M28 project.

My Responsibilities

  • Establishing and verifying survey control points.
  • Setting out and construction levels, road geometry and crossfalls.
  • Preparing survey information from approved road designs.
  • Supporting paving machine control.
  • Working with LandXML, road templates, TIN surfaces and RD3 data.
  • Supporting the preparation, verification and implementation of machine control data.

Software and Equipment

Topcon robotic total stations, GNSS, MAGNET Office, 3D-Office, Pocket 3D, AutoCAD and Civil 3D.
